Math Lessons & Progress

Digital math lessons and progress tracking for Lincoln International Academy

MathLIA is Lincoln International Academy's own platform for digital math lessons, grades 6 through 8 (including our Honors and Pre-AP tracks). Students complete lessons and practice online, and teachers track progress from a shared dashboard.

For students

Sign in with your school Google account to see your own grade's lessons and pick up right where you left off.

For teachers

Review class progress, flag items worth a closer look, and manage your roster from one dashboard.

School accounts only

Access is limited to Lincoln International Academy students and teachers signing in with their school account.
